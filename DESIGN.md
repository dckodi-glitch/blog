# Design

The site is a reading-focused field guide, with a large question-led opening
and a simple ruled index of the four projects. It does not visualize results
that have not been produced.

- Use deep blue ink and navigation, pale blue contextual fields, and a light
  reading background. Keep body text and links high contrast.
- Use the system sans-serif stack, a spacious reading measure, and clear
  hierarchy. Reserve monospace for code.
- Navigation and project links remain ordinary semantic links; the responsive
  menu must remain usable with a keyboard. Keep focus indicators visible.
- Figures need useful alternative text and captions; tables need meaningful
  headers and units. Do not rely on color alone for meaning.
- The empty archive describes its actual state. Do not display proposed or
  unassigned coverage slots as completed posts.
