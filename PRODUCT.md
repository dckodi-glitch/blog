# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Readers exploring the analyses are the primary audience. Portfolio reviewers
are a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

Explain four everyday decisions through reproducible, carefully dated Python
analysis: grocery prices, household electricity, fantasy football, and online
learning. A reader should be able to understand the question, evidence, limits,
and provenance without mistaking a proposed coverage slot for completed work.

## Operating Context

A Quarto site is to be deployed with GitHub Pages from the `blog` repository.
Writing and analysis together have an approximate two-hour weekly budget.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One featured project per weekly post; no fabricated posts, dates, or results.
- Keep future drafts, learner-level records, raw data, and private planning
  material out of the deployed site. Never use `studentVle.csv`.
- Static, curated outputs and exact code revision links accompany future
  analyses. The site build must not run analyses or download large datasets.
- Preserve original notebooks and raw data; exclude the original 2025 Week 8
  fantasy matchup analysis.

## Evidence on Hand

The planning artifacts in this checkout inventory source snapshots and
proposed coverage slots. There are no published weekly posts yet.

## Product Principles

- Separate observation, data vintage, execution, publication, and code dates.
- Describe uncertainty and scope without overstating what data establishes.
- Prefer small, reproducible analyses and readable, accessible explanations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide readable tables, captioned accessible figures, and navigable pages.
